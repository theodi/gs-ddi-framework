What is DDI & AI?

The DDI & AI framework brings together digital, data, innovation and AI as one picture of capability in government. It uses plain language at five maturity levels, from awareness through to expert, so you can see where you are today and what might come next.

What’s in the framework

The assessment covers four domains. Open a domain to read its clusters, skills and questions.

  • Digital

    Confident, safe and purposeful use of technology and leadership of digital change.

    4 clusters · 7 strands

  • Data

    Evidence-based decision-making through data literacy.

    4 clusters · 8 strands

  • Innovation

    User-centred, experimental and systems thinking skills, enabling continuous improvement.

    4 clusters · 7 strands

  • AI

    Understanding, applying and governing AI systems responsibly and sustainably.

    2 clusters · 6 strands

As well as the four domains, your report includes broader skills: five cross-cutting ways of working that sit alongside the domains.

How it works

  1. 1

    Complete your profile

    Your grade, role and, from EO upward, whether you mainly work as a subject expert or as someone who leads and supports others.

  2. 2

    Rate yourself by area

    One question at a time, filtered to what matters for your role. A few quick questions per domain keep the list relevant.

  3. 3

    View your progression report

    See maturity by topic area and those broader skills: a snapshot you can discuss with your manager or use for learning plans.
