      Complete your profile first

      We need your name, grade, role and organisation (and, from EO upward, how you mainly work) before opening domain questions. About two minutes.

      How this assessment works

      Three steps: refine what is relevant to your role, answer those questions, then build a report against the expectations for your grade.

      1. 1. Refine the questions

        Work area by area. A short pre-assessment for each domain selects the questions that fit your remit, so you are not asked everything in the framework.

      2. 2. Answer the questions

        Rate yourself against the level descriptors. You can skip anything that still does not apply after refinement.

      3. 3. Build your report

        Your answers roll into cluster scores. We compare those with focus bands: the proficiency range we expect for your grade and how you work. Broader skills (transversals such as communication or problem solving) are built from tags on the questions you answer across areas, not from a separate quiz.
