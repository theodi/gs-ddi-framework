DDI & AI skills and capabilities framework

The skills non-specialist civil servants need across digital, data, innovation and AI.

The Digital, Data, Innovation and AI (DDI & AI) Skills and Capabilities Framework sets out a clear, evidence-based view of the skills non-specialist civil servants need to develop across the four domains of Digital, Data, Innovation and AI.

It has been developed from national and international frameworks, applied evidence and engagement with Civil Service stakeholders.

How it is organised

Each domain is broken down into clearly defined skill clusters, grouping related skills. Each cluster includes individual skills.

All skills then map onto a five-level proficiency scale, from Novice to Expert.
