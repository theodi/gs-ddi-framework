Privacy

This publication is a static website. It does not ask you to create an account and it does not store assessments.

The pages and data files are public. Downloading JSON or CSV happens in your browser from this site.

The self-assessment prototype stores answers in your browser only. See that tool’s privacy page.

A full privacy notice will be published before any live government service.

If you have a question about how we use information, contact us.
